## 3. Playbook (house method; all untested in Gulf Arabic)

### 3.1 Tone and dialect rules
- Saudi/Gulf white dialect: "وش", "ليش", "الحين", "أبغى", "مره", "تقدر", "ترى". Not heavy classical, not forced slang.
- No em dashes, no emoji, no hype words ("ثورة", "سر", "لا يفوتك", "مضمون").
- Never claim results, customers, or numbers. Taleeq is not launched.
- Do not sound like a bot: vary sentence length, use contractions of speech, one idea per sentence. Avoid the stock AI openings ("في عالم اليوم").

### 3.2 Hook formulas (examples are my own)

1. **Reframe a small thing.** "[Word] مو [what you think], هذا [real meaning]."
   "«بكم؟» مو سؤال عن السعر بس."
2. **Instruction not to do the obvious.** "إذا [situation] لا [obvious action]."
   "إذا أحد كتب لك «غالي» لا ترد: يستاهل."
3. **Self-audit challenge.** "افتح آخر [N] [things] وعدّ [thing]."
   "افتح آخر عشرة تعليقات عندك وشوف كم واحد ردّيت عليه."
4. **Mini-scenario in second person.** "[Time]. [Person] كتب لك [short comment]."
   "الساعة عشر. واحد كتب لك «أبي»."
5. **Guess game.** "تعليق واحد، إما مجاملة أو عميل. خمن."
6. **Naming a tell.** "فيه ثلاث علامات تعرف منها إن الرد آلي."
7. **Reverse blame.** "المشكلة مو إن الرد آلي. المشكلة إنه باين."

### 3.3 Structure by duration

**15 s:** hook (0 to 3) / one point plus on-screen example (3 to 11) / one-question CTA (11 to 15).

**25 s:** hook (0 to 3) / the common belief (3 to 8) / the turn plus a concrete example (8 to 17) / the better move (17 to 22) / CTA (22 to 25).

**45 s:** hook (0 to 3) / scenario (3 to 10) / mistake and why it happens (10 to 20) / better move with example (20 to 32) / loop closes, one-line takeaway (32 to 38) / CTA (38 to 45). Place a mid CTA only in the A/B variant (see 2).

### 3.4 CTA patterns that produce comments Taleeq can learn from
- **Ask for a phrase:** "وش الكلمة اللي تحسّك تعرف إن العميل جاد؟" (collects intent language).
- **Ask for a number:** "اكتب رقم من صفر إلى عشرة." (cheap to answer, gives a baseline).
- **Ask for a real example:** "اكتب تعليق وصلك من غير أسماء." (raw material for the classifier; ask people to remove names and phone numbers).
- **Ask for a choice:** "تفضل ترد بالتعليق ولا بالخاص؟ وليش؟"
- Avoid: "علّق نعم", "منشن صديقك", "شير للفيديو".

### 3.5 What to avoid
- Ten scripts with the same arc. Alternate formats: myth-bust, scenario, list, quiz, teardown, challenge.
- Claims about results, speed, or sales. Illustrative screens must be labelled **مثال توضيحي**.
- Naming competitors negatively. Any competitor mention needs a source and date (file 01).
- AI-sounding delivery: flat rhythm, perfect parallelism, abstract nouns. Read aloud once; if it sounds like a brochure, rewrite.
- Telling people to send personal data in comments.

### 3.6 Faceless format kit
- Voice-over + burned-in Arabic text (RTL, large, 2 lines max) + screen recordings of a mock comment feed and mock DM thread.
- Every mock UI is labelled "مثال توضيحي". Real customer comments only with permission and names removed.
- Cut every 2 to 4 seconds; change the visual with every beat.
